# Startup animation assets

`default.mp4` is the Seedance-generated Cyrene animation supplied by the contributor for this feature. It is the original 1280×720, 24 fps, 15.041667 second silent video; no model, reference images or other source assets are distributed here.

SHA-256: `9ebb3f5204610f88d8e4c59052acbe098f08913ca7949dd2778f63c410a934d6`

Cyrene / 昔涟 and the depicted setting are characters and imagery from *Honkai: Star Rail*. Their underlying rights remain with their respective owners. This notice does not claim ownership of those characters or an official affiliation.

The Windows playback helper is derived from Dragon Codex Boot:
https://github.com/yushua0808-cpu/dragon-codex-boot

Its MIT notice is retained in `LICENSE-DragonCodexBoot`. Changes add Agent launch/attach modes, actual-media-duration playback, settings integration, frame holding, window conceal/restore and client-area alignment. The executable is rebuilt from `scripts/startup-animation/CyreneBoot.cs`; it is not committed as a binary. The helper uses the project's existing tray icon at build time.
